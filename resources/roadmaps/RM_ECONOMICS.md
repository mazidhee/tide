# Economics Roadmap

The economics a finance and payments engineer leans on: markets and incentives, money and central banks, interest rates and exchange rates, with the data to check each idea.

## 1. Microeconomics — CORE

### Markets

- [ ] Supply, demand and equilibrium
- [ ] Elasticity
- [ ] Market structure: competition, monopoly, oligopoly
- [ ] Two-sided markets and network effects (card schemes, platforms)
    - _The Economy — CORE Econ_

### Information and incentives

- [ ] Asymmetric information: adverse selection and moral hazard
- [ ] Principal and agent
- [ ] Game theory basics
    - _The Economy — CORE Econ_

### Practice

- [ ] Practice: Problem set, explain interchange fees as a two-sided market and predict the effect of a fee cap

## 2. Macroeconomics and money — CORE

### Output and prices

- [ ] GDP, inflation and unemployment
- [ ] The business cycle

### Money and central banks

- [ ] What money is, and who creates it
- [ ] Central bank balance sheets and reserves
- [ ] Policy rates, the transmission mechanism and the Taylor rule
- [ ] Quantitative easing
- [ ] Inflation and money financing
    - _Money creation in the modern economy — Bank of England Quarterly Bulletin 2014 Q1_
    - _Money financed government deficits and price levels — QuantEcon_

### Interest rates

- [ ] The yield curve and what it predicts
- [ ] Real and nominal rates, the Fisher equation
    - _Present values — QuantEcon_

### Practice
