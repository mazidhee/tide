# Tide user guide

The guide is one page per topic in [`guide/`](guide/), and the same pages are inside the app under **Help** (⌘?), with walkthroughs that play over screenshots of Tide.

## Get started

- [What Tide is](guide/01-welcome.md): A Mac app for learning deeply from your own material, with a tutor that questions you instead of lecturing.
- [Set up Tide](guide/02-setup.md): Install the app, point it at your notes and connect your model. About five minutes.

## Walkthroughs

- [A study day, start to finish](guide/10-flow-day.md): What a normal day in Tide looks like, from opening the app in the morning to wrapping up in the evening.
- [Learn a subject from zero](guide/11-flow-subject.md): From “I want to learn this” to a dated roadmap, a planned week and your first lesson.
- [Read a book you own](guide/12-flow-book.md): Tide maps every book in your vault, points you at the exact pages for what you're learning, and tests you on a chapter when you've read it.
- [Understand a system at work](guide/13-flow-system.md): Give Tide a design doc or spec. It maps the system's parts and flows, and teaches you each one using your system's own names and data.
- [Talk with your tutor](guide/14-flow-voice.md): Set up the natural voice once, pick who you hear, and have a spoken lesson without touching the keyboard.
- [Prepare for an interview](guide/15-flow-interview.md): Research the company, plan the prep, then run rounds that feel like the real thing and track your score.
- [Learn your Metabase](guide/16-flow-metabase.md): Connect Tide to your Metabase and ask the tutor what dashboards measure and how charts are built, with real numbers. Read-only, taught by Claude.

## Everyday

- [Home and your day](guide/20-home.md): One page that says what to do now, what comes after, and how the week is going.
- [Your week](guide/21-week.md): Your coach. It works out where you're heading and plans the next seven days to get you there.
- [Lessons and quizzes](guide/22-lessons.md): One question at a time, with a tutor that adjusts to every answer.
- [Notes and the tutor in them](guide/23-notes.md): Read and write Markdown, with a tutor that questions the paragraph you're on.
- [Notebooks and outlines](guide/24-notebooks.md): Every top-level folder in your vault is a notebook, with its own page, outline and reading shelf.
- [Flashcards and review](guide/25-review.md): Cards live in your notes, in the Obsidian Spaced Repetition format, and are scheduled with FSRS.
- [The Library](guide/26-library.md): Your reading list: books, papers, articles and videos, each a file in your vault's Library folder.
- [The Brain](guide/27-brain.md): Everything Tide knows about you and your learning, as one living map.
- [Practice](guide/28-practice.md): Learn by doing: coding challenges, interview problems, quant drills, system design and company prep.
- [Chat and memory](guide/29-chat.md): A full-page conversation with the tutor, which remembers lasting things about you.
- [Your tutor and voice](guide/30-voice.md): Who teaches you, what they sound like, and how you talk back.
- [The cloud helper](guide/31-cloud.md): Gemma on your Mac is always the tutor. A cloud model can help with four jobs where a bigger model makes a real difference.
